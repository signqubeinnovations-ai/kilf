## Your shop is judged in three seconds

A customer passing on the road gives your shop a glance. In that glance they decide: is this shop for me? Is it trusted? Is it open? The front elevation answers before your staff can say a word.

In busy Kerala towns, every shop competes with its neighbours for that glance. Most fronts look the same: a flex board, a shutter and a glass door. The shop that looks different, and looks cared for, gets the visit.

## What a designed shop front does

A good shop front elevation does three jobs.

1. **It is seen.** From the end of the street and from a moving vehicle, the front has one strong shape and colour.
2. **It is read.** The name and what you sell are clear, at the right size and height.
3. **It invites.** The entrance is obvious. The display is framed. The shop feels open.

ACP is ideal for this. Panels fold into box projections that frame a display window. Fins add rhythm along a wide front. Metallic and wood finishes signal quality. And the signboard becomes part of the architecture.

## Showrooms and retail we design for

- **Jewellery showrooms.** Metallic and champagne finishes, deep frames and controlled lighting.
- **Textile and fashion.** Wide glazing, warm wood finishes and a strong fascia.
- **Electronics and appliances.** Clean minimal fronts with bold brand colour.
- **Supermarkets and home stores.** Long fascias with clear signage and wayfinding.
- **Auto showrooms.** Large spans, crisp metallics and tall glazing.

## Designing your front

We visit, measure and photograph the shop and its street. We look at where customers come from, what they see first and how the light falls. Then we design concepts and show them in 3D on your actual building.

We design the signage at the same time. Letter sizes, logo placement, lighting and cable routes are all fixed in the design and shop drawings. Nothing is improvised on site.

## Building it without closing your shop

Panels and frames are prepared off-site to the drawings. On site, we plan the work in stages to keep your entrance usable. Where some work must happen outside business hours, we agree that with you in advance.

## Built to stay sharp

Shop fronts face the road: dust, traffic fumes, sun and heavy rain. We use exterior-grade coatings, stainless steel fixings and sealed joints. A simple wash keeps the front looking new.
