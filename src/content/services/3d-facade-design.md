## See it before you build it

A facade is a big decision. It costs real money and it will be on the street for years. Yet many owners approve a facade from a small sketch, or from a photo of someone else's building. Then they see the result for the first time when the scaffolding comes down.

We do it the other way round.

## What 3D facade design gives you

We build a 3D model of your actual building and design the new facade on it. You see:

- **Proportion.** How bands, fins and boxes sit against your floors and windows.
- **Finish.** Wood, stone, metallic and solid colours, side by side.
- **Signage.** Your name and logo at the right size, in the right place.
- **Context.** The building on its street, from the angles your customers see.
- **Day and night.** Where it helps, how the facade looks with its lighting on.

You can compare options, ask for changes and decide with confidence.

## Why we design and build

Many 3D elevations are drawn by someone who will never build them. They can look beautiful and be impossible, or very costly, to make.

Our designs are made by the same studio that fabricates and installs. Panel sizes, joints, returns and fixings are considered while we design. When you approve a view, we already know how to build it. Shop drawings are then prepared directly from the approved design.

## How the process works

1. **Photos and brief.** Send photos of the building and tell us what you need. We talk about brand, budget and timing.
2. **Survey.** We visit and measure every face that will change.
3. **Concepts.** We develop focused design directions in 3D.
4. **Refinement.** We refine your chosen direction until it is right.
5. **Samples.** You see physical finish samples, because screens vary.
6. **Approval.** You approve the final design. Then shop drawings and fabrication begin.

## Who it is for

- Shop and showroom owners planning a new front.
- Building owners comparing renovation options.
- Families designing a villa elevation.
- Architects who want a facade specialist to develop and detail an elevation.
