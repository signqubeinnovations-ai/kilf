## A commercial building is a sign you cannot switch off

A shopping complex, office block or hospital is seen by thousands of people a day. Its elevation tells them what to expect inside. Is this place modern? Cared for? Worth the visit?

Many commercial buildings in Kerala were built for function first. Plain plastered walls, a grid of windows, and signboards added later, tenant by tenant. Over time the building becomes a patchwork. Nobody planned it. Everybody sees it.

## What a designed elevation changes

A commercial elevation in ACP gives the whole building one clear idea. It can make a tall building look calmer with long horizontal bands. It can give a plain block depth with vertical fins and shadow. It can frame the entrance so visitors know exactly where to go.

It also brings order to signage. When the elevation includes a planned signage zone, every tenant gets a fixed place and size. The building stays clean as tenants come and go.

## Built for the type of building

- **Shopping complexes.** Strong street presence, a clear entrance and a signage system that works for many brands.
- **Corporate offices.** Restrained materials, precise lines and your brand colour used with care.
- **Hospitals.** Calm, clean finishes, clear wayfinding, and fire-retardant cores as standard.
- **Educational campuses.** Durable surfaces, a recognisable identity and low maintenance across many blocks.

## Designing the elevation

We begin with a survey and full measurement of every face. We study the structure, the existing windows, drainage and services. Then we develop concepts and show them in 3D on your building, from the street and from the approach road.

When the design is approved, we prepare shop drawings and a framework plan. Panel sizes are set to suit the windows and floor heights, so joints line up with the building, not against it. This is where an elevation goes from good to precise.

## Fabrication and installation

Our team fabricates the panels to the drawings and installs the aluminium framework and panels on site. For larger buildings we plan the work in stages, so entrances, parking and business continue. Joints are sealed for monsoon rain. Lines are checked level and plumb as the work goes up.

## Safety and compliance

For taller and public buildings, the panel core matters. We recommend fire-retardant (FR) or A2-grade cores and state the exact core in the quote. Framework anchors are chosen for the wall and wind exposure of the site. If structural input is needed, we say so early.
