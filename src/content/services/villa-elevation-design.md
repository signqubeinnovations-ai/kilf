## A home should look the way it feels inside

Families in Kerala put years of savings and care into a home. Yet many elevations end up as an afterthought: a few projections, some tiles, paint chosen late. The house works well inside but says little outside.

A villa elevation is the first thing your guests see and the last thing you see each night. It is worth designing.

## What ACP brings to a villa

Used with restraint, ACP gives a home crisp modern lines that plaster cannot hold for long. It suits Kerala's weather when detailed well: aluminium does not rust, good coatings hold their colour, and the panels shed rain.

Common villa uses:

- **Box projections** that frame windows and balconies.
- **Feature walls** in wood or stone finish beside the entrance.
- **Parapets and fascias** with clean, straight edges.
- **Porch ceilings and columns** that stay neat for years.
- **Gate pillars** that match the house.

## Wood and stone without the upkeep

Wood-finish ACP brings the warmth of timber with no termites, warping or polishing. Stone-finish panels give the look of weight and permanence at a fraction of the load on your structure. We usually pair one warm finish with one calm solid colour. Two or three finishes, used with intent, look far better than ten.

## Modern, but at home in Kerala

A good Kerala home deals with sun, heavy rain and humidity. We keep that in mind: deep projections for shade, simple shapes that shed water, and finishes that will not look tired after a few monsoons. The elevation should look modern now and still look right in ten years.

## How we design your elevation

We visit the home, or study the drawings if it is under construction. We talk about how your family uses the house, your taste and your budget. Then we design the elevation and show it in 3D on your home, from the gate and from the road.

After approval, we prepare shop drawings and our team fabricates and installs the facade. We protect your garden and finishes while we work, and leave the site clean.

## New build or makeover

Joining during construction lets us plan fixing points and window details from the start. For existing homes, we fix the new facade on a framework in front of the old wall, with no demolition. See [facade renovation](/acp-renovation) for how that works.
