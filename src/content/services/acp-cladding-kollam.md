## Why buildings in Kollam need a better face

Walk down any main road in Kollam or Karunagappally. Most buildings look tired long before their structure is old. Paint fades in one summer. Monsoon stains run down from every ledge. Signboards are added one on top of another until the building disappears behind them.

Customers notice. A shop, clinic or office is judged in seconds, and a worn front tells them to keep walking. Repainting fixes it for a year. Then the rain comes back.

## What ACP cladding does

ACP cladding puts a new skin on the building. Aluminium composite panels are fixed on an aluminium framework in front of the existing wall. The wall stays. The face changes.

Done well, the result is a flat, precise surface with straight joints and a finish that holds its colour. Panels can be folded into trays, wrapped around corners and shaped into fins, bands and boxes. The same system carries your signage and lighting.

ACP is also light. That matters for older buildings, where heavy stone or tile cladding would add too much load.

## Details that decide how long it lasts

Most ACP failures we see in Kerala are not panel failures. They are detail failures.

- **Framework.** Aluminium sub-frames do not rust. We plan spacing and anchors for the wall type and wind exposure.
- **Fixings.** We use stainless steel fasteners that stand up to coastal humidity.
- **Joints.** Open or badly sealed joints let water behind the panels. We detail joints and drainage for driving monsoon rain.
- **Coating.** We specify exterior-grade coatings meant for strong sun, so colours hold.
- **Core.** For public and taller buildings, we quote fire-retardant cores and tell you exactly which core you are getting.
