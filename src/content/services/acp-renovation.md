## Same building. New face.

Many buildings in Kerala are structurally sound but look tired. Decades of sun and monsoon have stained the walls. Ledges collect black streaks. Old signboards hang at different heights. The building is worth more than it looks.

Rebuilding is expensive and slow. Repainting lasts a season. There is a third option.

## Renovation without demolition

An ACP facade renovation puts a new skin in front of the old wall. We anchor an aluminium framework to the existing structure and fix ACP panels on it. The old wall stays in place behind. The building stays in use.

The result looks like a new building from the street. Clean surfaces, straight lines, a clear entrance and signage that belongs.

## What we check first

A new facade is only as good as what holds it. Before we design, we check:

- **Structure.** Where the framework can be anchored safely, and what the wall is made of.
- **Dampness and leaks.** Leaks must be fixed first. A facade hides leaks but does not cure them.
- **Drainage.** Where rainwater comes off the roof and ledges, so the new facade sends it away.
- **Services.** AC units, pipes, cables and meters that must stay accessible.
- **Windows and ventilation.** Which openings stay, change or close.

We tell you plainly if something needs repair before facade work begins.

## Designing the new face

We photograph and measure the building, then design the renovation in 3D on your actual building. You can see before and after side by side. We plan signage, lighting and AC unit screening as part of the design.

Old buildings rarely have perfectly straight walls. Our framework absorbs that, so the new face is level and plumb even when the old wall is not.

## Working while the building is in use

Facade renovation happens outside. We plan scaffolding, work zones and timing so shops, offices and tenants keep working. Panels are prepared off-site, which keeps the time on site short.

## Good reasons to renovate the facade

- To attract better tenants or buyers.
- To launch a rebrand or a new business in an old building.
- To stop repainting every year.
- To bring order to years of mismatched signage.
